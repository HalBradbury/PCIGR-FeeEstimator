What happens next

  1. Save or copy your estimate above.

This is an estimate, not a quotation. Prices are subject to change without notice; confirm all costs with us before committing funds.

We are a research facility, not a commercial laboratory. Some materials cannot be run here, among them highly mineralised rock compositions, metals and organics, and work may be declined. Please ask before planning around an estimate.
